Comprehensive Privacy Policy & Data Protection

Last updated: August 6, 2026. Please read this comprehensive privacy policy carefully to understand how we collect, process, protect, and utilize your personal information, user credentials, and ad identifiers.

1. Preamble & Scope of Policy

Welcome to Haryana Tools (managed and operated under JK Enterprises). We respect your privacy and are committed to safeguarding the personal information and data you share with us. This Privacy Policy details our practices concerning data collection, account credentials management, cookie tracking, and third-party monetization networks (including Google AdSense) across our e-commerce catalog, blog, and digital utilities.

2. User Accounts, Authentication & Login Data

To access specialized features such as order processing, checkout, coupon redemptions, saved address books, and interactive blog commentary, users create an account on our platform. In connection with these services, we collect and securely process:

  • Personal Identity Data: Full legal name, phone number, email address, and physical shipping/billing addresses.
  • Authentication Credentials: Usernames and heavily encrypted/hashed password strings stored within protected database schemas.
  • Session Identifiers: Secure JWT (JSON Web Tokens) or session IDs stored via browser local storage or HTTPS cookies to maintain active login states safely.
  • Transaction Records: Historical order data, invoices, return logs, and cart configurations linked directly to your authenticated profile.

3. Technical Telemetry, Log Files & Browser Caching

Like most modern web platforms, Haryana Tools collects standard server log data and client-side performance metrics. This data includes Internet Protocol (IP) addresses, user-agent details, browser types, Internet Service Providers (ISP), date/time stamps, referring/exit pages, and click paths. We utilize this information exclusively to analyze traffic patterns, diagnose infrastructure issues, mitigate cybersecurity threats, and optimize local storage and CDN performance.

4. Google AdSense, DoubleClick DART Cookies & Monetization

To offer free access to our instructional tool guides, solar maintenance articles, and technical blog publications, Haryana Tools integrates third-party advertising frameworks, primarily Google AdSense.

By browsing our website, you acknowledge and agree to the following third-party advertising conditions:

  • Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies and web beacons to serve personalized advertisements based on your prior visits to our website or other sites on the Internet.
  • Google's use of advertising cookies (such as the DoubleClick DART cookie) enables it and its partners to serve targeted ads based on your browsing habits across the web.
  • Users may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting the Google Ads Settings page or by visiting aboutads.info.

5. Cookies, Local Storage & Tracking Technologies

We employ essential cookies, performance cookies, and browser local storage mechanisms. Essential cookies are required to manage basic platform architecture (such as persisting shopping cart items, verifying login sessions, and routing dynamic JS modules). Analytics and ad-network cookies enable us and third-party vendors to serve relevant content and monitor aggregate usage metrics. Users can manage or disable cookie preferences directly through their individual browser settings.

6. Data Retention, Security & Integrity Protocols

We employ strict technical, physical, and administrative measures to secure your personal information against unauthorized access, loss, or alteration. All transaction channels are encrypted via SSL/TLS protocols. Account passwords are encrypted prior to database insertion. While we implement industry-standard encryption practices, no system can guarantee absolute immunity against cybersecurity vulnerabilities; users remain responsible for maintaining the secrecy of their credentials.
