Whether you're buying your first angle grinder or upgrading your existing tool, these frequently asked questions will help you choose, use, and maintain your angle grinder safely.

---
What is an angle grinder used for? An angle grinder is commonly used for: - Cutting metal - Grinding welds - Removing rust - Surface preparation - Sharpening tools - Tile and stone cutting - Concrete cutting - Polishing with suitable accessories
Which angle grinder size is best? - **100 mm (4")** — Home users and DIY projects - **115 mm (4.5")** — General workshop use - **125 mm (5")** — Professional fabrication work - **180 mm (7")** — Heavy-duty industrial work - **230 mm (9")** — Concrete and structural steel cutting Choose the size according to the type of work rather than simply selecting the largest grinder.
What motor power should I choose? - **650–850 W** — Household work - **900–1200 W** — Workshop and fabrication - **1400 W+** — Continuous industrial applications Higher wattage is recommended only for demanding jobs.
What is the difference between a grinding disc and a cutting disc? A **grinding disc** is thicker and designed for removing material. A **cutting disc** is thinner and should only be used for cutting. Never use a cutting disc for grinding.
Can an angle grinder cut stainless steel? Yes. Use an **INOX-rated cutting disc** designed specifically for stainless steel.
Can I cut ceramic tiles or marble? Yes. Use a **diamond blade** suitable for ceramic, porcelain, marble, or granite. For cleaner cuts: - Mark the cutting line. - Make shallow passes. - Avoid forcing the blade.
Can an angle grinder cut wood? Standard abrasive discs should **never** be used on wood. If wood cutting is required, use only blades specifically designed and approved for angle grinders.
Is a cordless angle grinder better than a corded model? **Cordless** - Portable - Ideal for outdoor work - Suitable where power is unavailable **Corded** - Continuous operation - Higher sustained power - Better for heavy-duty applications Choose based on where and how often you'll use the tool.
What safety equipment should I wear? Always wear: - Safety goggles or face shield - Hearing protection - Dust mask when required - Cut-resistant gloves - Safety shoes Avoid loose clothing and jewellery.
Why is my angle grinder vibrating? Common causes include: - Damaged disc - Incorrect installation - Bent spindle - Worn bearings - Poor-quality accessories Stop using the grinder immediately if excessive vibration occurs.
When should grinding or cutting discs be replaced? Replace a disc immediately if it is: - Cracked - Chipped - Warped - Excessively worn - Damaged after being dropped - Past its expiry date Never use damaged abrasive discs.
Can an angle grinder remove rust? Yes. Popular rust-removal accessories include: - Wire cup brushes - Wire wheels - Flap discs - Grinding wheels Choose the accessory based on the amount of rust and required finish.
How do I maintain an angle grinder? Regular maintenance includes: - Cleaning air vents - Inspecting the power cord - Tightening the guard - Checking carbon brushes - Cleaning the spindle - Storing the tool in a dry place Proper maintenance improves performance and extends tool life.
How long does an angle grinder last? A quality angle grinder can provide years of reliable service when: - Used correctly - Maintained regularly - Fitted with quality accessories Actual lifespan depends on workload and operating conditions.
Can beginners use an angle grinder safely? Yes. Beginners should: - Read the instruction manual - Wear appropriate PPE - Use the correct disc - Hold the tool firmly with both hands - Practice on scrap material before actual work
What accessories are commonly used with angle grinders? Common accessories include: - Cutting discs - Grinding wheels - Flap discs - Diamond blades - Wire cup brushes - Wire wheels - Sanding pads - Polishing pads Always ensure accessories are compatible with the grinder's disc size and speed rating.
Which angle grinder brand should I choose? Compare brands based on: - Build quality - Motor power - Safety features - Warranty - Spare parts availability - Service network - Availability of accessories Choose a model that matches your intended applications rather than only the price.
