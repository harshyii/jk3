Bench Grinder Buying Guide: How to Choose the Right Bench Grinder

A bench grinder is one of the most useful workshop machines for sharpening, grinding, polishing, deburring and restoring metal tools. Whether you're a fabricator, mechanic, carpenter or DIY enthusiast, choosing the correct bench grinder improves accuracy, safety and productivity.

What is a Bench Grinder?

A bench grinder is a stationary grinding machine with two rotating grinding wheels mounted on either side of an electric motor. Different wheels can be fitted for sharpening, polishing, buffing or cleaning various materials.

Common Applications
  • Sharpening chisels
  • Sharpening drill bits
  • Grinding welding joints
  • Removing rust
  • Knife sharpening
  • Metal deburring
  • Surface polishing

Safety Tips

Always follow these safety practices before using a bench grinder.

  • Wear safety goggles or a face shield.
  • Use hearing protection during extended operation.
  • Keep the tool rest close to the grinding wheel.
  • Inspect grinding wheels for cracks before use.
  • Never exceed the wheel's rated speed.
  • Allow the grinder to reach full speed before grinding.
  • Keep loose clothing away from moving wheels.
  • Use light, steady pressure while grinding.

Maintenance Tips

Clean Regularly

Remove grinding dust frequently to improve cooling and motor life.

Dress the Wheel

Use a wheel dresser to maintain a flat grinding surface.

Check Bearings

Replace noisy bearings immediately to reduce vibration.

Inspect Guards

Ensure wheel guards and eye shields remain securely fitted.

Buying Checklist

Before purchasing a bench grinder, check that it includes:

  • Correct wheel size
  • Suitable motor power
  • Heavy-duty construction
  • Low vibration design
  • Adjustable tool rests
  • Eye protection shields
  • Reliable warranty
  • Easy spare parts availability
