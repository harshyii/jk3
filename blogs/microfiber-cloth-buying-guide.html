Microfiber Cloth [Buying Guide](blog.html?id=car-shampoo-buying-guide): How to Choose the Best Cleaning Cloth

Microfiber cloths have become an essential cleaning accessory for homes, workshops, garages and professional detailers. They remove dust, grease, fingerprints and water spots without scratching delicate surfaces. However, not every microfiber cloth is made the same. GSM, weave type, edge stitching and fiber blend all affect performance. This guide explains everything you should know before buying microfiber cloths.

Why Choose Microfiber?

Unlike ordinary cotton cloths, microfiber is made from ultra-fine polyester and polyamide fibers that trap dust instead of pushing it around.

Understanding GSM

GSM (Grams per Square Meter) indicates the thickness and absorbency of a microfiber cloth.

GSM Best For Performance
200-250General cleaningLightweight
300-350Kitchen & HomeBalanced
400-500Car detailingHighly absorbent
600+Polishing & DryingPremium

For everyday cleaning, a 300–400 GSM cloth provides the best balance between softness and durability.

How to Maintain Microfiber Cloths

  • Wash separately from cotton towels.
  • Use mild detergent only.
  • Avoid fabric softeners.
  • Do not use bleach.
  • Air dry whenever possible.
  • Store clean cloths in a dust-free container.

Buying Checklist

Before buying, check for:

  • Suitable GSM
  • Soft edge stitching
  • Premium microfiber blend
  • Lint-free finish
  • Machine washable quality
  • Reusable construction
  • Trusted manufacturer
  • Good customer reviews
