What is an On Grid Solar System?

An On Grid Solar System (also known as a Grid-Tied Solar System) is connected directly to the electricity distribution network. During the day, electricity generated by solar panels powers your building first. Any surplus electricity is exported to the utility grid through a net meter.

At night or during cloudy weather, electricity is automatically imported from the grid. Since batteries are not required, on-grid systems are generally more affordable than hybrid or off-grid solar systems.

How Does an On Grid Solar System Work?

1. Sunlight

Solar panels convert sunlight into DC electricity.

2. Solar Inverter

The inverter converts DC power into usable AC electricity.

3. Home or Business

Generated electricity is consumed instantly by connected electrical loads.

4. Excess Power

Unused electricity is exported back to the electricity grid through net metering.

Maintenance Tips

Clean Solar Panels

Dust, leaves and bird droppings reduce efficiency. Clean panels regularly using soft water and a non-abrasive cloth or brush.

Inspect Wiring

Ensure all electrical connections remain secure and protected from moisture or physical damage.

Monitor Power Generation

Track daily energy production through the inverter display or monitoring application to identify abnormal performance.

Annual Inspection

Have the complete solar system inspected periodically to ensure optimal efficiency and safety.
