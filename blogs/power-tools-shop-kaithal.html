Power Tool Safety Tips

Always inspect your machine before use and wear appropriate personal protective equipment (PPE).

  • Wear ISI-approved safety goggles.
  • Use hearing protection during prolonged operation.
  • Inspect power cords before every use.
  • Never operate damaged grinding discs.
  • Use the correct accessory for each machine.
  • Disconnect power before changing accessories.
  • Keep children away from the work area.
  • Read the manufacturer's instruction manual.

Maintenance Tips for Longer Tool Life

Keep Tools Clean

Remove dust after every project to improve cooling and prevent premature motor wear.

Lubricate Moving Parts

Regular lubrication reduces friction and extends gearbox life.

Replace Worn Accessories

Old cutting wheels and drill bits reduce efficiency and increase risk.

Store Properly

Keep tools dry and protected from moisture to avoid corrosion.
