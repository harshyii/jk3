Net Metering Explained

One of the biggest advantages of an on-grid solar system is Net Metering. During the daytime, your solar panels may produce more electricity than your building consumes. Instead of wasting this extra energy, it is exported to the electricity grid through a net meter.

Simple Example

  • Your home uses 12 units during the day.
  • Your solar system generates 20 units.
  • The extra 8 units are sent to the electricity grid.
  • These exported units help reduce your future electricity bill according to applicable utility policies.

Maintenance Tips

Clean the Panels

Dust, bird droppings and leaves reduce energy generation. Clean panels periodically using soft water and a non-abrasive cloth.

Check for Shade

Growing trees and nearby construction may create shadows that reduce system efficiency.

Monitor Performance

Use inverter monitoring to detect any unexpected reduction in electricity generation.

Annual Inspection

Inspect wiring, earthing, mounting bolts and protection devices every year.
