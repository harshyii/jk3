Maintenance Tips for Longer Tool Life

Clean After Every Job

Remove dust, concrete residue and metal particles to improve cooling and motor efficiency.

Inspect Carbon Brushes

Replace worn brushes before they damage the motor commutator.

Lubricate Moving Parts

Proper lubrication reduces wear and improves overall machine performance.

Store Properly

Keep machines in dry, dust-free storage cases to prevent rust and electrical damage.

Safety Checklist

Always follow these basic safety practices before operating any power tool.

  • Wear certified eye protection.
  • Use hearing protection during extended work.
  • Inspect power cables before use.
  • Use the correct accessory for the machine.
  • Secure the workpiece before cutting or drilling.
  • Disconnect power before changing accessories.
  • Never remove factory-installed safety guards.
  • Keep children and bystanders away from the work area.
