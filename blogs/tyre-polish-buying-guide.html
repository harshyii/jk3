How to Choose the Best Tyre Polish for Long Lasting Shine

By JK Enterprises • 2026-05-15

Tyre Polish [Buying Guide](blog.html?id=car-shampoo-buying-guide): How to Choose the Best Tyre Shine for Your Vehicle

A quality tyre polish does much more than make your tyres look glossy. It protects rubber from UV rays, slows fading and cracking, repels dirt and water, and gives your vehicle a fresh, well-maintained appearance. This guide explains everything you should know before buying a tyre polish.

Why Use Tyre Polish?

Tyres are constantly exposed to sunlight, rain, mud and road chemicals. Without protection, rubber gradually loses its deep black colour and begins to dry out.

Benefits
  • Restores deep black finish
  • Protects against UV damage
  • Prevents premature fading
  • Repels dust and dirt
  • Enhances vehicle appearance
  • Extends tyre life

Non-Greasy Formula

How to Apply Tyre Polish

① Wash the tyres thoroughly.
② Allow the rubber to dry completely.
③ Apply polish using a foam applicator.
④ Spread evenly across the sidewall.
⑤ Allow it to dry for a few minutes.
⑥ Wipe away excess product if required.

Common Mistakes

  • Applying polish on dirty tyres.
  • Using excessive product.
  • Applying on the tyre tread.
  • Driving immediately after application.
  • Using solvent-based products regularly.

Buying Checklist

Before purchasing a tyre polish, check for:
  • Water or silicone based formula
  • UV protection
  • Long-lasting finish
  • Easy application
  • Non-greasy formulation
  • Suitable for all tyre types
  • Trusted manufacturer
  • Good customer reviews
