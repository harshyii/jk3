Return & Refund Policy

We want you to be completely satisfied with your tools. Review our industry-leading return guidelines below.

📦 Extended 200-Day Return Policy

We offer an extended 200-day return window for items that are completely new and unused. If you change your mind or bought a tool by mistake, you can return it within 200 days of delivery.

Please Note: A restocking and handling charge of 20% will be deducted from the refund amount for all returns processed under this extended window. Items must be in their original packaging, un-opened, and in pristine, resalable condition.

🛠️ Defective or Damaged Items

If you received a defective, damaged, or incorrect tool, this policy does not apply. Defective items reported within 7 days of delivery qualify for an immediate, full 100% refund or free replacement with zero restocking charges.

🔄 How to Initiate a Return

Follow these simple steps to start your return:

  1. Log in to your account and navigate to your Order History.
  2. Select the order containing the item and click on Request Return.
  3. Confirm that your item is new, unused, and packed in its original box.
  4. Our pickup executive will schedule a collection from your address. Once inspected at our warehouse, your net refund (after the 20% deduction) will be processed.
